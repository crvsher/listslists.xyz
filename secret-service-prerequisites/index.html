secret service prerequisites

It’s not that hard.

  • U.S. citizen
  • Age 21-37 (up to 40 for veterans)
  • Valid driver's license
  • Excellent health and physical condition
  • Bachelor's degree, great grades
  • OR 1 year of graduate-level education
  • OR 3 years of specialized experience in investigations or law enforcement
  • Vision: 20/100 uncorrected, correctable to 20/20
  • Hearing: No more than 25-decibel loss in either ear
  • Pass physical abilities test
  • Qualify for Top Secret clearance
  • Pass background investigation
  • Pass polygraph examination
  • Clean drug test
  • No visible body markings (tattoos, etc.)
  • Willing to accept assignments anywhere in the U.S. and overseas
  • Strong communication skills
  • Ability to make quick decisions under pressure
  • High ethical standards and discretion
  • Teamwork abilities
